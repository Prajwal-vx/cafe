Weekend Escapes

Ready-Made Weekend Road Trips in Koshi

Combine coffee, scenic drives, and mouthwatering local cuisine with these handpicked weekend trails.

Scenic Hills & Mist Trail

Dharan to Bhedetar Cloud Escape

Escape the lowlands for crisp mountain fog, panoramic views of the Koshi valley, and Dharan's famous sekuwa.

  • 1
    Morning: D Best Coffee (Dharan) Single-origin Himalayan Arabica & fresh croissants at Bhanu Chowk.
  • 2
    Lunch: LevelUp Restro (Vijayapur) Spectacular hill terrace view with momos and iced americanos.
  • 3
    Late Afternoon: Bhedetar Cloud Nine & Charles Point Watch rolling clouds engulf the ridge while sipping spiced hot chocolate.
Tea Gardens & Mountain Air

Ilam & Kanyam Green Retreat

A serene weekend journey through endless emerald tea bushes, cool breezes, and organic orthodox teas.

  • 1
    Midday: Kanyam Cloud View Cafe Fresh first-flush orthodox tea overlooking infinite tea estates & spicy akabare.
  • 2
    Evening: Ilam Heritage Tea & Coffee House Heritage wooden architecture, tea-tasting flights, and local chhurpi snacks.
  • 3
    Sunday Dawn: Antu Valley Sunrise Cafe Unforgettable sunrise view of Mt. Kanchenjunga with hot ginger-honey mountain coffee.
Urban Gourmet & Garden Hangouts

The Jhapa & Sunsari Cafe Crawl

Perfect for urban explorers, foodies, and remote workers who appreciate stylish design and wood-fired ovens.

  • 1
    Brunch: Mimiz Cafe (Birtamod) Pastel-toned aesthetic brunch with avocado toast and matcha lattes.
  • 2
    Afternoon: Oisi Coffee House (Damak) Damak’s favorite barista stop for artisan cappuccino and velvety tiramisu.
  • 3
    Saturday Evening: Brikshya Cafe (Birtamod) Botanical sanctuary, live acoustic music, and authentic wood-fired pizzas.
