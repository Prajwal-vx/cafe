What this site stores

Koshi Cafe Finder has no account, submission form, analytics, or advertising system. It does not send your saved cafes or theme preference to the site operator. Your wishlist and light or dark theme choice are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. You can clear them through your browser's site data settings.

Third-party requests

The page requests fonts and styles from Google Fonts and cafe photographs from images.unsplash.com. Those services receive the request information needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address, browser information, and requested resource. Opening a cafe's map link sends your search to Google Maps. Calling a cafe uses your device's phone handler. These services have their own privacy practices.

Hosting and security

The web host may process standard connection logs, such as IP address, request time, and requested file, under its own privacy terms and retention settings. This project does not control those logs. The site uses HTTPS when served by a host configured with a valid HTTPS certificate.
